Our Projects

Roofs, Ramps & Restorations

A few recent jobs from our crew: a new tile roof, ramps that make getting home easier, and old woodwork brought back to the way it was built.

Residential · Tile Roof

A New Tile Roof

A stucco home with a low, many-hipped roofline, finished in flat tile that suits its arches and columns, with the skylight and chimney worked in cleanly.

  • Flat tile on every hip and valley
  • Skylight and chimney flashed in
  • Gutters and trim left clean

Accessibility · Ramps

Ramps That Make Home Easier

When a family member needs a safer way in and out, we build it fast: long wooden ramps with railings on both sides and landings at the turns, often alongside a bathroom remodel inside.

  • Built on site from treated lumber
  • Handrails on both sides
  • Landings where the ramp turns

Historic · Restoration

Old Woodwork, Brought Back

Older homes are built differently, so we match what is already there: dark-stained trim, paneled columns, staircases and hardwood floors restored to look the way they always did.

  • Original woodwork kept and refinished
  • Hardwood floors restored
  • Stairs, doors and trim repaired to match
